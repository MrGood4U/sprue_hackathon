It may render validated candidate sources, selected query entities, field bindings, stable node IDs, and explicit edges, but its `requires_source_admission` status is not a compiled or executable version. A missing, failed, clarification, unsupported, or older proposal without this projection opens a blank editable draft. Do not describe planning output as live query/runtime evidence or repeat activity as cohort retention.
